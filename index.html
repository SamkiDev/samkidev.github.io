    01About

    An emerging software engineer, always learning

    I'm Samalani Maluwa (SamkiDev), a student pursuing a BSc in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of South Africa (UNISA). I'm early in my journey: still learning, sharpening my technical skills and gaining practical software development experience one project at a time.

    I'm passionate about software engineering, full-stack development, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. I put that into practice through personal projects, academic work and collaborative technology initiatives such as hackathon teams, and I completed the FNB App Academy Full Stack Development programme in 2025.

    AI-powered coding assistants are part of how I work. They help me explore solutions, understand unfamiliar technologies, debug issues, review code and work more efficiently. I see AI as a development tool and a learning companion, not a replacement for the fundamentals: I make the effort to understand and test what I build, and I take responsibility for the code I put my name on.

    I'm curious, adaptable and comfortable picking up new tools, and I'm excited about where software development is heading. My aim is to keep improving as a developer and to learn how to build software that is useful, reliable and meaningful.
